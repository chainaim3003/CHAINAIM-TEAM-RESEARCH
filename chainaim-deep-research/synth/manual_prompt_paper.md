## Write, in Markdown, in this order

3. **Method** — the key idea / architecture / approach, in plain words.

5. **Limitations** — stated by the authors, plus anything the text leaves open.
6. **Terms to know** — key concepts introduced, one line each.
7. **Relevance** — who should read it and why (only from what the paper says).
